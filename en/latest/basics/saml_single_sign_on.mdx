---
title: "SAML single sign-on for Invoca"
description: "Connect an identity provider such as Okta, Azure, or ADFS to Invoca using SAML single sign-on so users can log in with their existing corporate credentials."
---

Invoca allows for single sign-on to be orchestrated by an identity provider by utilizing the SAML protocol. Documentation for configuring your SAML single sign-on integration can be found [here](https://community.invoca.com/t5/platform-administration/saml-single-sign-on-appendix/ta-p/627).
