## Gaps in the current rules

- **Focus containment, focus return, and keyboard dismissal are unconfirmed at the Titan
  layer.** The one published test only checks that the Drawer renders and shows its content; it
  does not exercise closing, focus, or keyboard behavior.
- **A bare Drawer with no `footerContent` is the only pattern demonstrated in the one published
  story besides the default render.** Whether a footer with no border of its own (it does carry
  a `background-default` fill — see [Drawer](/invoca-design-system/components/containment/drawer#anatomy))
  is an accepted look, or a gap the product works around per-instance, is unrecorded.
- **Whether a left-, top-, or bottom-anchored side panel is needed anywhere in the product, and
  is being hand-built outside this component as a result, has not been checked.**
